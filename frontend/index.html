Animal awareness

Add an ID tag and microchip

A collar tag with your phone number and a registered microchip are the fastest ways to get a lost pet home.

Keep recent photos

Clear photos from the front and side, plus any markings, make a report far easier to match.

Found an animal?

Stay calm, keep it safe, take a photo and post it here. Then check with a nearby vet for a microchip scan.

Adopt, don't abandon

Pets need years of time, food and care. If you can't keep one, contact a shelter instead of leaving it behind.

Common questions

I lost my pet. What should I do first?

Search the area right away, post a report with a clear photo and location, tell neighbours, and call nearby vets and shelters.

I found an animal. Is it safe to keep it?

Keep it safe and calm for now, take a photo, post a found report and ask a vet to scan for a microchip. Don't feed unfamiliar food to an injured animal.

How do I close my report?

Log in, open your report and choose "Mark as reunited" once your pet is home.

Is PetConnect free?

Yes. Reporting, searching and adoption listings are free for everyone.

Emergency?

If an animal is injured, contact a local veterinarian or animal rescue helpline immediately. This site is not an emergency service.
